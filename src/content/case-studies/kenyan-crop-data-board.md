  Kenyan crop/agricultural figures, modelled in Power BI Desktop.

  Shaped the data in Power Query, built a star-friendly model, and designed
  report pages that let a reader explore crops and trends interactively rather
  than crawling source tables.
outcomes:
  - 'An interactive Power BI data board over the crop dataset.'
  - 'A layout designed for exploration: filters and visuals that answer questions in a few clicks.'
